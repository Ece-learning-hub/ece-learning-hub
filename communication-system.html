1. Introduction to Communication Systems

A communication system is used to transfer information from one place to another.

Examples: Mobile phones, television, radio and internet.

Telugu: Oka place nundi inko place ki information pampinchadaniki use chese system ni Communication System antaru.

2. Basic Blocks of Communication

  1. Information Source: Creates the message.
  2. Transmitter: Prepares and sends the signal.
  3. Channel: Medium through which the signal travels.
  4. Receiver: Receives and processes the signal.
  5. Destination: Gets the final information.
Source → Transmitter → Channel → Receiver → Destination

3. Signals

Analog Signal

An analog signal changes continuously with time. Example: Traditional radio audio signals.

Digital Signal

A digital signal represents information using discrete values, commonly 0 and 1. Example: Digital data in computers.

4. Modulation

Modulation is the process of changing a property of a carrier signal according to the information signal.

Types of Analog Modulation

  • AM: Amplitude Modulation.
  • FM: Frequency Modulation.
  • PM: Phase Modulation.
Telugu: Information signal ni carrier signal tho combine chesi transmit cheyadaniki modulation use chestaru.

5. Demodulation

Demodulation is the process of extracting the original information signal from the received modulated signal.

Example: A radio receiver extracts audio information from a received radio signal.

6. Antennas

An antenna is a device used to transmit or receive electromagnetic waves.

Applications:

  • Mobile communication
  • Satellite communication
  • Radio and television
  • Wireless networks

7. Applications of Communication Systems

  • Mobile phones
  • Wi-Fi and Bluetooth
  • Satellite communication
  • Television broadcasting
  • Radio broadcasting
