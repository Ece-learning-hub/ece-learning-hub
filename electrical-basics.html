1. Introduction to Electrical Engineering

Electrical engineering deals with electricity, electrical circuits and electrical devices.

Telugu: Electricity ni generate cheyadam, transmit cheyadam mariyu use cheyadam gurinchi chadivedhe Electrical Engineering.

2. Voltage

Voltage is the electrical potential difference between two points.

Unit of Voltage = Volt (V)

Telugu: Voltage anedhi current flow avvadaniki driving force laga pani chesthundhi.

3. Electric Current

Electric current is the rate of flow of electric charge.

Current = Charge / Time

Unit of Current = Ampere (A)

Telugu: Electric charge flow avvadanne current antaru.

4. Resistance

Resistance is the opposition offered to the flow of electric current.

Unit of Resistance = Ohm (Ω)

Telugu: Current flow ni oppose chese property ni resistance antaru.

5. Ohm's Law

Ohm's Law states that current is directly proportional to voltage when temperature and other physical conditions remain constant.

V = I × R
I = V / R

V = Voltage, I = Current, R = Resistance

Example:

Voltage = 12V
Resistance = 4Ω
Current = 12 / 4 = 3A

Telugu: Voltage ni resistance tho divide chesthe current value vasthundhi.

6. AC and DC

AC - Alternating Current

AC changes its direction periodically. Example: Household electricity supply.

DC - Direct Current

DC flows in one direction. Example: Battery supply.

Telugu: AC direction maruthu untundhi. DC okate direction lo flow avuthundhi.

7. Electrical Power

Electrical power is the rate at which electrical energy is used or transferred.

P = V × I
Unit = Watt (W)

Example: 12V × 2A = 24W

8. Series and Parallel Circuits

Series Circuit

Components are connected one after another. The same current flows through each component.

Parallel Circuit

Components are connected across the same two points. The voltage across each branch is the same.

Telugu: Series lo components okati tarvatha okati connect chestham. Parallel lo separate branches ga connect chestham.
